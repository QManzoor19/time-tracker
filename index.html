How to actually keep this up

Three check-ins a day, not continuous logging. Lunch, dinner, and bed. Each takes ~20 seconds: open the day, pick a category, drag over the blocks you just lived. Recall over a 4–6 hour window is reliable; recall over a whole day is not — that's the entire reason for check-ins rather than an end-of-night entry.

Log the leak honestly or don't log it at all. The one number that changes behaviour is the one you're tempted to round down. A day that reads "3.5h drift" is a useful day.

Judge on the week, never the day. Single days are noise — a bad Tuesday means nothing. Look at Insights on Sunday, change one target, and leave the rest alone.
